OUR FOUNDING VISION

Redefining clinical nutrition through empirical science

Stackly was established in 2014 by a team of clinical dietitians and medical researchers frustrated by trend-driven, ineffective dietary advice. We set out to build an institution where biochemistry, microbiome profiling, and human behavioral psychology work hand-in-hand.

Over the past decade, we have helped more than 15,000 individuals reverse metabolic dysfunction, regulate cardiovascular biomarkers, and achieve sustainable weight balance without deprivation.

Our mission, vision and clinical values

Our Clinical Mission

To eradicate chronic lifestyle-driven metabolic diseases through personalized precision nutrition, clinical patient advocacy, and actionable culinary education.
