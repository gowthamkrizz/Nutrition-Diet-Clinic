FREQUENT QUESTIONS

Service details & insurance questions

Everything you need to know about starting care with Stackly.

Does health insurance cover Stackly clinical consultations?

Many major health insurance plans (including Aetna, BlueCross, and Cigna) provide full or partial reimbursement for Medical Nutrition Therapy (MNT) under preventative care benefits. We provide itemized superbills with clinical diagnostic codes for all sessions.

Can I switch plans or cancel at any time?

Yes, all memberships operate on month-to-month terms without locked contracts. You can pause, adjust, or cancel your membership with a single click inside your patient dashboard.

What happens if I have severe food allergies or intolerances?

Our clinical dietitians specialize in IgE food allergies, celiac disease, histamine intolerances, and FODMAP sensitivities. Every single recipe is strictly screened against your allergy profile.

Are the video consultations private and secure?

Yes, our telehealth video system is fully HIPAA-compliant and protected by 256-bit end-to-end encryption. Your medical records, biometric data, and notes remain strictly confidential.
