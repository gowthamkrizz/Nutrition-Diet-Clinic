FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know before your visit

Quick answers about our consultation intake, telehealth requirements, lab reviews, and personalized care roadmap.

What should I prepare prior to my first clinical consultation?

Please gather any recent blood diagnostic reports (lipid panel, fasting blood glucose, HbA1c, thyroid levels, kidney enzymes) conducted within the last 6 months, along with a 3-day log of your typical dietary habits. You can upload these directly via email or present them at reception.

How do Telehealth HD Video consultations work?

Once your appointment is confirmed, you will receive an encrypted video link via SMS and email. No app downloads are required—simply click the link from your smartphone, tablet, or laptop browser to enter the private consultation room with your lead dietitian.

Does Stackly assist with health insurance coverage or wellness claims?

Yes. We provide complete itemized clinical receipts and medical nutrition therapy super-bills with standardized ICD-10 medical coding for preventive care or chronic disease management reimbursement through major private insurers.

How soon after the intake do I receive my personalized nutrition plan?

Your custom therapeutic nutrition blueprint, rotating meal templates, pantry staples guide, and targeted macro parameters are published to your patient portal within 24 to 48 hours following your diagnostic review.
