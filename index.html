CLEAR ANSWERS

Answers to your clinical nutrition questions

Timeline & Results
How long until I notice meaningful results?

Most patients observe improved digestion and sustained energy within 7 to 10 days. Measurable body composition changes and metabolic laboratory biomarkers consistently improve within 4 to 8 weeks of consistent adherence.

Consultation Prep
What should I bring to my initial consultation?

For your first appointment, bring any recent bloodwork or laboratory panels (within the past 6 months), a list of current medications and supplements, and a 3-day log of your standard eating habits.

Individual Care
Do you create customized meal plans individually?

Yes, absolutely. We do not use generic one-size-fits-all meal plans. Every recipe, macro ratio, and ingredient is curated around your allergies, cultural preferences, kitchen schedule, and clinical targets.

Demanding Lifestyle
Will my nutrition plan fit busy professional schedules?

Our plans are engineered specifically for demanding routines. We provide 15-minute quick-prep meal frameworks, healthy dining-out guides, travel strategies, and streamlined weekly batch-cooking instructions.

Athletics & Recovery
Do you offer clinical support for athletic performance?

Yes, our sports nutrition specialists design nutrient-timing protocols, glycogen recovery frameworks, and electrolyte hydration strategies for competitive athletes and marathon runners.
